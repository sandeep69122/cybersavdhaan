Essential Cyber Safety Tips

Stay alert, stay informed and stay safe online. Follow these essential tips to protect yourself from cyber threats.

10 Essential Safety Tips

Simple habits that can help keep you safe in the digital world.

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for every account. Avoid using your name, birthday or common words. Consider using a trusted password manager.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available. It adds an extra layer of protection to your online accounts.

3. Never Share OTP or PIN

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV or passwords with anyone. Banks and genuine support staff should never ask you to reveal these secrets.

4. Beware of Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links received through SMS, email or social media. Check website addresses carefully before entering personal information.

5. Follow Safe UPI Practices

Verify the receiver's name before paying. Remember, you do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

6. Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid accessing banking accounts or making sensitive payments on unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

7. Check App Permissions

Download apps from trusted sources and allow only the permissions they genuinely need. Remove apps you no longer use.

8. Keep Devices Updated

Regularly update your phone, computer, browser and applications. Updates often include important security fixes.

9. Protect Your Social Media Privacy

Review your privacy settings. Avoid publicly sharing sensitive personal details, financial information or your live location.

10. Report Cyber Fraud Quickly

If you face financial cyber fraud, contact your bank immediately and report it by calling 1930 or using the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
