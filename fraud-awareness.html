Cyber Fraud Awareness

Learn about common online scams, recognize warning signs and take steps to protect your money, identity and personal information.

Know the Scams. Stay Safe Online.

Cyber fraud can happen to anyone. Scammers use fake messages, calls, websites and social media accounts to trick people. Awareness is your first line of defence.

1. UPI Payment Fraud

Scammers may send fake payment requests or trick people into entering their UPI PIN. Never enter your PIN to receive money.

2. OTP Scam

Fraudsters may pretend to be bank or service representatives and ask for your OTP. Never share OTPs, passwords or PINs.

3. Phishing & Fake Links

Fake emails, SMS messages and websites may imitate trusted organizations to steal your login or banking information.

4. Fake Job Scams

Fraudsters may offer fake jobs and demand registration, training or security fees. Verify employers before paying.

5. Online Shopping Fraud

Fake shopping websites and social media sellers may take payment without delivering products. Check seller credibility.

6. Digital Arrest Scam

Scammers may impersonate law enforcement and threaten arrest through video calls to demand money. Real authorities do not conduct arrests or demand payments this way.

7. Social Media Account Hacking

Fake login pages and suspicious links can steal account credentials. Use strong passwords and two-factor authentication.

8. Lottery & Prize Scams

Messages claiming you have won a prize may ask for advance fees or personal details. Do not pay to claim an unexpected prize.

Important Safety Warning

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV or banking password. Do not install unknown apps or allow screen sharing at the request of an unknown caller.

What to Do If You Become a Victim of Cyber Fraud?

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately to report the transaction.
  2. Call India's cybercrime helpline at 1930 as soon as possible if money is lost.
  3. Report the incident at the official portal: cybercrime.gov.in.
  4. Save transaction IDs, screenshots, phone numbers and messages as evidence.
  5. Change compromised passwords and secure affected accounts.
