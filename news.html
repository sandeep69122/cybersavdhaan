UPI Payment Scams: How to Protect Your Money in India

CyberSavdhaan Desk | Cyber Safety Awareness | Updated November 2026
📱

UPI fraud awareness: Digital payments have made money transfers easier, but scammers also use fake payment screenshots, fraudulent collect requests and impersonation tactics to target people.

1. How Does UPI Fraud Happen?

Fraudsters may send fake payment screenshots to shopkeepers, pretend to be bank representatives or trick people into approving unknown UPI collect requests.

Some scammers also send suspicious links claiming that users must click them to receive money or complete a refund.

2. Common Warning Signs

  • Unknown UPI payment requests.
  • Fake payment confirmation screenshots.
  • Calls asking for your UPI PIN or OTP.
  • Suspicious refund or cashback links.
  • Pressure to transfer money immediately.
Important Safety Alert: You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Never approve an unknown payment request.

3. How to Protect Yourself

Always verify incoming payments directly in your official banking or UPI application. Never rely only on screenshots or SMS messages.

Do not share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or confidential banking information with anyone.

4. What to Do If You Become a Victim?

If you lose money due to online financial fraud:

  • Immediately call the national cyber fraud helpline 1930.
  • Report the incident at the official cybercrime portal.
  • Inform your bank or payment service provider.
  • Preserve transaction IDs, screenshots and messages.
