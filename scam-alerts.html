Cyber Scam Alerts

Stay alert. Recognise online scams. Protect your money and personal information.

⚠️ UPI Payment Request Scam

Scammers may send fake payment requests or QR codes and trick people into entering their UPI PIN.

Stay safe: Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Check every payment request carefully.

⚠️ Fake Customer Care Numbers

Fraudsters may post fake customer support numbers online and ask for OTPs, bank details or remote access.

Stay safe: Find customer care details only on the company's official website or app.

⚠️ Fake Job Offers

Scammers promise easy jobs or high salaries and demand registration fees or other payments.

Stay safe: Verify the employer and never pay money just to secure a job.

⚠️ Phishing Links and Messages

Fake messages may claim that your bank account, SIM card or KYC will be blocked unless you click a link.

Stay safe: Avoid unknown links. Visit the official website directly instead.

⚠️ Digital Arrest Scam

Fraudsters may pretend to be police or government officials and threaten people through video calls to demand money.

Stay safe: Do not transfer money because of threats over a call. Verify through official authorities.

⚠️ Fake Online Shopping Deals

Fraudulent websites and social media pages may advertise products at unusually low prices and disappear after receiving payment.

Stay safe: Check the seller's reputation, website details and payment options before ordering.

⚠️ Lottery and Prize Scams

Messages may claim that you have won a lottery or prize and ask you to pay a fee to receive it.

Stay safe: Do not pay money or share personal details to claim an unexpected prize.

🚨 What to Do If You Become a Victim

1. Contact your bank immediately to report the transaction and seek account protection.

2. Call India's cyber fraud helpline 1930 as soon as possible.

3. Report the incident on the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
