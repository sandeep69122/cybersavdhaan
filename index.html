Essential Cyber Safety Tips

🔐

Protect Your OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN or passwords with anyone.

🔗

Check Every Link

Avoid unknown links received through SMS, email or WhatsApp.

📲

Secure Your Apps

Download apps only from trusted official sources.

💳

Secure Your Payments

Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
